How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your WiFi Monitor Pro account and all associated data using either of the following two options:

  • Option 1 (In-App Deletion): Open the WiFi Monitor Pro app on your Android device → Navigate to Profile / Account Settings → Select Delete Account & Data → Confirm deletion.
  • Option 2 (Web Form Request): Enter your registered email address in the form below and click Submit Account Deletion Request. Your account and associated data will be deleted immediately from our backend servers.

Data Types Deleted Upon Request

  • Account Identity & Credentials: Email address, User ID, authentication credentials, display name, and password hashes.
  • Support & Communication History: Support tickets, feedback messages, and customer service inquiries.
  • Diagnostics & Technical Logs: Network test session records, analytics events, and associated diagnostic logs linked to your user account.

Data Retention & Policy

Upon account deletion, all user data stored on our backend servers is permanently purged. No user personal data or account records are retained after deletion processing is completed (0-day retention post-deletion).
