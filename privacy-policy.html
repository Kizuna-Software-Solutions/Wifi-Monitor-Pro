1. Information We Process

WiFi Monitor Pro processes the technical information needed to provide its network diagnostic features. This may include:

  • SSID and BSSID of wireless networks.
  • IP address and network information required for connectivity and latency diagnostics.
  • Wi-Fi signal and other technical measurements required for network analysis.
  • Technical information associated with tests and application performance.

2. Local Processing

Network information used by WiFi Monitor Pro is processed on your device for its core diagnostic functions. We do not intentionally upload SSIDs, BSSIDs, Wi-Fi scan results, or network measurements to Kizuna Software Solutions' servers for advertising or profiling.

AI Analytics: AI-powered analytics are used to analyze network performance and provide insights within the Application. The AI feature is designed to process the required technical data securely and is not used for advertising, profiling, or selling user information.

3. Help and Support & Crash Logs

We collect crash logs only to investigate issues, debug errors, and improve Application performance and stability. WiFi Monitor Pro does not use third-party tracking services or sell your data. If you need help or support, you may choose to share application logs or diagnostic information with Kizuna Software Solutions through the support feature or by email.

Any information you choose to send to us is used only to investigate issues, provide support, and improve the Application. We do not request or require you to share logs unless they are needed to help resolve a reported issue.

4. Advertising and Tracking

WiFi Monitor Pro is ad-free. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use the Application for cross-app advertising or behavioral tracking. We do not intentionally collect or use the Google Advertising ID for advertising purposes.

5. Permissions and Storage

The Application may request Android permissions required for Wi-Fi scanning, network diagnostics, or features you choose to use. Permissions can be managed through your device settings.

The Application may use local storage required for normal operation. This storage is not intended for advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information handled by WiFi Monitor Pro. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Policy Updates
