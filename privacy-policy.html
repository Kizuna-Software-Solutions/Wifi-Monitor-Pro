At Kizuna Software Solutions, we built WiFi Monitor Pro with a strong commitment to user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our Application interacts with your data, what information is processed to deliver our network diagnostic services, and how we protect your digital footprint.

By installing and utilizing WiFi Monitor Pro, you acknowledge and agree to the data practices described in this document. This policy forms a part of our overall commitment to transparency and operates alongside our standard Terms and Conditions.

1. Data Processing and Core Functionality

We believe in minimal data overhead. To deliver real-time network insights—such as speed testing, signal heatmapping, DFS monitoring, and deployment analysis—the Application requires operational access to specific network data.

Localized Technical Data

To analyze your network environment, the Application processes:

  • Service Set Identifier (SSID): To identify your current wireless network connection.
  • Basic Service Set Identifier (BSSID): To detect specific wireless access points.
  • Internet Protocol (IP) Address: To run standard routing and latency diagnostics.

Our Local-First Principle: All network-specific data listed above is processed entirely on your physical device to generate your diagnostics. This information is never transmitted to, stored on, or harvested by our external corporate servers.
