About WiFi Monitor Pro

Welcome to WiFi Monitor Pro, developed by Kizuna Software Solutions. Built for everyday users, network enthusiasts, and IT deployment professionals alike, WiFi Monitor is your all-in-one suite for network diagnostics, optimization, and security.

Our Philosophy

High-fidelity network tools shouldn't be locked behind paywalls or ruined by intrusive tracking. That is why the entire application—including all advanced Enterprise and Pro diagnostic tools—is 100% free and completely ad-free.

💻 Home Workspace (Core Features)

The Home environment is built to optimize your everyday wireless experience:

  • Access Points: Visualize nearby signals and find the least crowded channel for your router.
  • Speed Test: Run precise latency, upload, and download diagnostics.
  • Devices (Who is on my WiFi?): Audit all connected hardware and detect unauthorized intruders in real time.
  • AI Assistant: Get instant, smart recommendations and guided troubleshooting steps.
  • Security: Run automated encryption sanity tests and vulnerability checks.
  • History: Track network performance trends with a detailed log of past speed tests.
  • WiFi Info: View real-time metadata including RSSI, IP configuration, and frequency bands.

🏢 Enterprise Workspace (Advanced Tools)

For network engineers, IT administrators, and infrastructure specialists, our Enterprise Tier unlocks professional-grade telemetry suites at zero cost:

  • Spectrum: Deep frequency analysis—channel overlapping, DFS channels, and environmental interference.
  • Quality: Continuous packet-delivery audits, jitter calculations, and professional QoS evaluations.
  • Neutral Host Manager: Seamless multi-operator connectivity and indoor deployment for shared cellular-to-Wi-Fi offloading.

Our Promise

Whether you are securing a home router or auditing corporate infrastructure, WiFi Monitor Pro delivers enterprise-grade insights with a strict focus on data privacy and local-first processing. No ads, no hidden costs, no compromised data.
