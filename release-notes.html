WiFi Monitor Pro - Completely Rebuilt & Reimagined

Welcome to WiFi Monitor Pro

Welcome to the all-new WIFI MONITOR PRO (formerly WiFi Monitor) – completely rebuilt from the ground up with a modern, intuitive UI designed for network professionals, IT administrators, and everyday users who demand absolute control over their wireless environments.

🛑 IMPORTANT: WiFi Monitor Pro is proudly 100% AD-FREE. No banner ads, no forced videos, and no data tracking. Just pure, professional-grade diagnostic utility.

⚡️ DUAL INTERFACE PROFILES

We've split the app experience to perfectly match your environment:

  • HOME SECTION: Clean, intuitive tools for optimizing household coverage, managing connected family devices, and running instant sanity checks.
  • ENTERPRISE SECTION: Deep-dive diagnostics, spectrum intelligence, and advanced network auditing built for enterprise-grade environments.

🚀 POWERFUL NEW MODULES & FEATURES

  • Devices (Who is on my WiFi?): Scan and identify every phone, smart TV, and rogue IoT device connected to your network in real time.
  • NH Manager: Dedicated Neutral Hosting management tools built to streamline cellular-offload and shared infrastructure deployment.
  • AI Assistant: Your personal network engineer. Ask real-time questions, analyze complex logs, and get simple, actionable troubleshooting steps.
  • Advanced Security: Real-time forensic checks including legacy cipher flags, Protected Management Frames (PMF) analysis, and automated risk scoring.
  • Sharing App Feature: Easily transfer configurations, network profiles, or app access seamlessly across local devices.
  • History & Sharing Test Results: Track your network's health over time and export high-fidelity test results instantly to clients, colleagues, or support teams.

📊 PRO SPECTRUM MONITORING

Keep track of 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz channel congestion, visualize channel bonding parameters, and leverage intelligent automated recommendations to dodge interference effortlessly.
