The App can be used without an account. If you created or signed in to an account (for the AI assistant or worker tasks) you can request deletion of it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@kizunasoftwaresolutions.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my Spatial AI account".
  2. We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account profile and sign-in identifier.
  • AI usage-credit records tied to the account.

What may be kept

  • Survey data you already submitted to an organisation through worker tasks belongs to that organisation; contact them or us to have it removed.
  • Records we must keep by law.

Data stored only on your phone is not on our servers; remove it by deleting maps in the App or uninstalling it.
