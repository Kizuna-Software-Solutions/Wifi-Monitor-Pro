Spatial AI WiFi Heatmaps • Kizuna Software Solutions
Last updated: October 10, 2026

Spatial AI WiFi Heatmaps ("the App", package com.kizunasoftwaresolutions.spatialai) lets you import a floor plan, walk the space, and record WiFi and cellular signal measurements to build a heatmap. This policy explains what the App processes and why.

1. Information the App processes

  • Signal measurements: WiFi signal strength (RSSI) and estimated SNR of the connected network, and cellular signal values (RSRP/RSRQ) of the serving cell, recorded at points along your walked trail.
  • Movement and position: camera frames (for ARCore motion tracking), accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer and step sensors, used to estimate where you are on your floor plan.
  • Floor plans and photos: images or PDFs you import, and photos you capture and pin to the map.
  • Location and nearby-device permissions: Android requires location (and Nearby devices) permission to read WiFi and cellular signal information. The App does not use these to track where you are in the world.
  • Account information (optional features): if you sign in to use the AI assistant or worker tasks, your account identifier and sign-in credentials are handled by our authentication provider.

2. Local storage

Maps, trails, measurements, photos and settings are stored on your device. Exported reports and files are created only when you choose to export or share them, and you decide where they go.

3. AI assistant

If you use the in-app AI assistant, your message, recent conversation, and a short summary of the current map's measurements are sent through our server (Supabase) to an AI model provider (Groq) to generate a reply. The provider key is kept on our server, never in the App. AI requests require sign-in and use a usage-credit system. Data sent to the assistant is used only to answer your question; we do not use it for advertising or sell it.

4. Worker tasks (Kensetsu)

If you sign in with a Kensetsu worker account and choose to submit a survey, the floor-plan survey data you submit (map geometry and measurement points) is sent to the Kensetsu service operated by Kizuna Software Solutions for the organization that assigned the task. Nothing is submitted unless you submit it.

5. Advertising and tracking

The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information and do not collect the Google Advertising ID.

6. Permissions

  • Camera: ARCore tracking and photo markers.
  • Physical activity: step counting.
  • Location / Nearby devices: required by Android to read WiFi and cellular signal data.
  • Bluetooth / Nearby: optional device-to-device sharing between nearby phones.
  • Internet: AI assistant and worker-task features.
  • Foreground service / battery optimization: keeps tracking running while the screen is off during a mapping session.

You can change permissions at any time in your device settings.

7. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers described above (hosting/authentication and the AI model provider) to operate those features, or when required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. See Delete Data and Delete Account for how to remove data held on our servers.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change when we do.
