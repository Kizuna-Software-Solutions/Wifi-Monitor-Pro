Data on your device

  • Delete a single map: open Saved Maps and tap the delete (bin) icon on the map.
  • Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → Spatial AI → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the App.
  • Files you exported or shared live where you saved them; delete them there.

Data on our servers

Only data from the optional AI assistant and worker-task features reaches our servers. To delete it, email support@kizunasoftwaresolutions.com from your account email. We complete requests within 30 days. To delete the account itself, see Delete Account.
